# Concepts, glossary and 500 questions: implementation plan

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-concepts-and-500-design.md`. Execution: inline for code, parallel agents for content and design, the same as round 1.

## Global constraints

The same as round 1: no libraries, plain style (no em dashes, no semicolons), no emojis, 375 px first, reduced motion respected, noreply commits with no AI attribution, nothing pushed without the owner's go-ahead.

## File formats

- `content/glossary/catalogue.json`: `[{ "id": "precision", "name": "Precision", "topic": "evaluation", "diagram": false }]`. Ids are lower-case kebab-case.
- `content/glossary/<topic>.json` holds an array of entries. Each entry has `id`, `name`, `topic`, `aka` (list), `summary` (8 to 35 words), `body` (60 to 180 words, markup), `mixup` (optional, 12 to 70 words), `related` (1 to 5 ids), `diagram` (optional id) and `asof` (optional).
- `content/glossary/diagrams/<id>.svg`: `viewBox` set, no width or height, colours only through `currentColor` or `var(--ink)`, `var(--muted)`, `var(--line)`, `var(--right)`, `var(--wrong)`, `var(--sheet)`, `var(--paper)`. Allowed elements: svg, g, path, rect, circle, ellipse, line, polyline, polygon, text, tspan, title, defs, marker.
- Questions gain `concepts`: 1 to 4 catalogue ids. This is required once Task 7 has tagged the first 90.
- `content/blueprint-round2.json`: round 1 slot shape plus `concepts`.
- The build writes `data/bank.json` and `data/glossary.json` as `{ "version": 1, "concepts": [entry with "svg" inlined] }`.

## Tasks

1. **Catalogue and blueprint (agent).** About 250 concepts covering all 14 topics, about 40 marked `diagram: true`. 410 new slots with the topic split from the spec and the round 1 mix rules. The mix is about 40% mcq, 12% multi, 15% fill, 18% numeric and 15% open, with difficulty about 30% easy, 45% medium and 25% hard. No duplicate of a round 1 angle. Each slot names 1 to 4 catalogue concepts.
2. **Build support (TDD).** Extend `tools/build.py`: validate glossary entries, sanitize diagrams, check `concepts` ids, and write `data/glossary.json`. Make `concepts` required behind a flag until Task 7 is done.
3. **Schedule and store (TDD).** Add `saveConcept`, `saveTerm`, `removeSaved` and `isSaved` in a new `assets/js/saved.js`. Allow the optional `saved` field in `isProgress`. Add `activityByDay`, `coverage`, `dueForecast` and `byDifficulty` in `assets/js/insights.js`.
4. **App wiring.** Concept buttons after Check, the `#concept/<id>` view, the Concepts tab (My list, Save a term, searchable glossary) and four tabs. Then plain functional versions of the new Progress and Settings content.
5. **Design pass (design agent).** Polish the Concepts, Progress and Settings pages and the concept card, in the round 1 visual language.
6. **Glossary content (agents).** Entries by topic group, diagrams by a separate agent, then a fact-check review agent per group.
7. **Tag the first 90 (agent)** with `concepts`, then turn on the required flag.
8. **410 questions in waves (agents).** Wave size is about 5 writers of about 25 questions each. After each wave, blind review plus critique (one reviewer per writer), then a second blind solve on changed keys, then build and commit.
9. **Final.** Full tests, a browser walkthrough at 375 px and on desktop, one fresh code review, rulings, hand over.
